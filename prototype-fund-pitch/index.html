Evidence-Gated Agents

Hello, I’m Robert Schaub. Facts and evidence are an essential foundation for democracy, justice and good business decisions. I’m working on Evidence-Gated Agents. Before AI acts, it must have permission and enough evidence to justify the action.

I spent 39 years in software engineering and project leadership. I founded FactHarbor, a Swiss non-profit association. I’ve built two separate projects: the FactHarbor fact-checking application. It assesses claims against supporting and opposing evidence. And Our AI Charter Runtime, an early prototype. It checks permission and evidence before allowing an AI action. With funding, I will be able to build and test a working Evidence-Gated Agents prototype using these two foundations.

Here is a practical example: replacing oil heating with an air-to-water heat pump. Can this be generally recommended without first checking the building’s specific conditions? This FactHarbor report weighs the evidence. Here you can inspect the evidence and its source.

Now, in the Evidence-Gated Agents prototype, a user submits a question or claim. The AI drafts an answer using evidence from FactHarbor reports. A separate gateway will check current authority and evidence for that exact answer. The gateway can allow or block the answer, or ask a human reviewer to narrow or decline the request. Every revision must pass all checks again. If the answer is released, it comes with a receipt you can inspect. It links the answer, authority, evidence, reasoning, decision and outcome.

Beyond the prototype, I want to develop evidence-gated decision-making applications and services. With permission, a future product could also gather and use an organisation’s non-public evidence. Consequential AI-based decisions must always be open to inspection and challenge.

I want to work with the Prototype Fund’s coaches and participants to challenge my assumptions and develop the project further. Through its network, I’d like to involve potential users in testing the prototype.

My vision is a free and fair society where consequential decisions must remain authorised, grounded in evidence and open to challenge.
