# Organisation

The FactHarbor Verein supports a non-profit, open-source project for transparent, evidence-based claim analysis. The [published statutes](https://github.com/robertschaub/FactHarbor/blob/main/Docs/Legal/Vereinsstatuten_FactHarbor_DE.md) establish the association's bodies and responsibilities.

## Governance and participation

![Governance and operational responsibilities](../diagrams/diagram-2f6b502a73c6a990.svg)

[Full-size diagram](../diagrams/diagram-2f6b502a73c6a990.svg) · [Mermaid source](../diagrams/diagram-2f6b502a73c6a990.mmd)

The diagram is an organisational model. It does not establish that every operational role is staffed or that planned community capabilities are available. Formal authority follows the statutes and adopted policies.

- [Governance](governance/index.md): responsibility for the system, evidence-based improvement and intervention boundaries.
- [Contributor processes](how-we-work-together/contributor-processes/index.md): proposing, checking and reviewing changes.
- [Legal and compliance](legal-and-compliance/index.md): effective policies, licensing and contribution terms.
- [About FactHarbor](../about-factharbor/index.md), [cooperation](../product-development/presentations/index.md) and [support](../funding.md).

FactHarbor remains an Alpha. The project aims to make reports and the reasoning behind them inspectable, while respecting privacy and access restrictions. Non-profit status alone does not guarantee analytical neutrality or quality.
