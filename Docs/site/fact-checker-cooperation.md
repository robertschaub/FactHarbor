# FactHarbor for fact-checkers and newsrooms

FactHarbor is a non-profit, open-source project for evidence-based claim analysis. It researches text and articles, separates verifiable assertions, and produces reports with supporting and opposing evidence, reasoning and uncertainty.

## What a newsroom can inspect

![Evidence research and structured challenge support an inspectable report](diagrams/fact-checker-cooperation-1.svg)

[Full-size diagram](diagrams/fact-checker-cooperation-1.svg) · [Mermaid source](diagrams/fact-checker-cooperation-1.mmd)

Evidence can support different conclusions under different conditions. FactHarbor's analytical boundaries make those conditions visible; its debate method challenges an assessment using documented evidence. The report should let a reader examine the sources and understand why a conclusion was reached.

This can support pre-publication research, identification of evidence gaps and teaching how an assessment is constructed. Editorial judgment, original reporting and specialist image/video verification remain separate responsibilities. An automated report is not a substitute for checking the cited material.

## Status and limitations

The invite-gated Alpha remains available at [app.FactHarbor.ch](https://app.factharbor.ch). Broader development is paused pending funding. Reliability, repeatability, multilingual consistency and resource efficiency need further evaluation. No fixed turnaround time or uniform accuracy is promised.

A report may miss relevant sources, misinterpret evidence or express too much confidence. Truth assessment and confidence are distinct; weak or conflicting evidence needs visible qualification. Citations help scrutiny but do not guarantee correctness. Model allocation depends on the active configuration, so a static presentation does not establish which providers ran a particular analysis.

## Possible cooperation

A useful first discussion would identify one workflow problem, what evidence would demonstrate improvement, and a manageable evaluation scope. Newsrooms could contribute workflow knowledge and assessment of report usefulness; FactHarbor can offer an Alpha application and inspectable outputs. Access, data handling, resources and publication arrangements must be agreed before a study or pilot starts.
