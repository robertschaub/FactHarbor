**Key decisions (diagnosis of the three candidate causes, as revised after review):**
- **Cause 1, wording admits a narrow true reading — supported as the largest single-run effect; dominance not established.** Same build: the VBS-page sentence lands at MOSTLY-TRUE 72 % with EFK and GPK-N content in the pool (R3 AC_01); the sharpened wording lands at 30 % (R1) and 52 % (R2). Production with the original wording: 65 % and 88 %. One run per condition, article context in R3, an unverified production runtime state, and a decomposition confound (R2's `expectedEvidenceProfile.primaryMetric` emphasises the price amount, R1's the guarantee and inflation exclusion; R2 also had a failed atomicity-validation call) mean the wording effect is not separated from retrieval and decomposition. Mechanically the routing is by prompt design: `VERDICT_ADVOCATE`/`VERDICT_RECONCILIATION` judge only the stated proposition and send omitted qualifications to `misleadingness`. The flag captured the omission in every run (R1/R2 highly, R3 potentially, production highly/potentially). `misleadingness` exists per claim only (`CBClaimVerdict`); there is no article-level field or aggregation (`aggregation-stage.ts`), but the verdict narrative's key finding also states the mismatch in R1–R3 and is rendered in the headline banner, so the claim badge is not the only carrier. R2 split the conjunctive claim into "price agreed" (true) and "binding, inflation-excluding" (false) and averaged to MIXED.
- **Cause 2, retrieval dominated by government pages repeating the claim — not reproduced locally; the observed retrieval failures are structural.** Counting `admin.ch` (all subdomains), `parlament.ch`, `.gov` and `.mil` in raw results gives 8 of 42 (R1) and 60 of 254 (R3); `www.admin.ch` evidence items were 0/2/3 in R1/R2/R3, 1/2/8 including subdomains, versus 25 of 38 official communications reported for production. This neither explains nor refutes production's mix (provider order and prompt blob there are unverified per `Docs/STATUS/Current_Status.md`). Code-confirmed mechanisms that removed decisive sources locally:
  1. `claim-selection-filter.ts` `filterClaimUnderstandingForSelectedClaims` (line 86) deletes preliminary items whose `claimId`/`relevantClaimIds` do not match selected claim ids, before the remap/seed fallbacks in `research-orchestrator.ts` run; introduced with automatic claim selection in `d2d06f839` (2026-05-24). Observed: `understanding.preliminaryEvidence` empty and `seededEvidenceItems` 0 in all four runs and all 28 claim ledgers, while the 4/3/6/4 preliminary extraction calls succeeded. Why no item matched is inferred, not observed: the extractor is not given the claim ids, but the `EXTRACT_EVIDENCE` output example carries `"relevantClaimIds": ["AC_01"]`, so matching by imitation is possible; pre-filter payloads are not logged. Preliminary evidence still shapes Pass 2 (`expectedEvidenceProfile`, `groundingQuality`); it is lost only as evidence.
  2. `research-acquisition-stage.ts` `fetchSources` (line 173) skips URLs already in `state.sources`, which Stage 1 populates; the later source reconciliation (line 522) repairs metadata and does not re-extract. R1: admin.ch `nsb?id=90403` ranked 0.90–0.95 in four Stage 2 fetch lists and was never extracted; the 13 Aug 2025 release likewise. R3: nzz.ch GPK-N article re-selected twice, never extracted against AC_01.
  3. `RELEVANCE_CLASSIFICATION` conflated relevance with agreement in one refuting pass (R2, `2026-09-09T09:15:18Z`): five of seven results whose own reasoning says "contradicts" scored 0.10–0.20 and the floor (`relevanceFloor ?? 0.4`, `research-extraction-stage.ts:206`) dropped them, including the 13 Aug 2025 release that scored 0.95 in R1. This is one episode in 74 discard blocks across the runs; recurrence is unverified. Shared exposure: `classifyRelevance` passes neither iteration intent nor `expectedEvidenceProfile`, although the prompt's relevance rule refers to that profile (`research-extraction-stage.ts:116`, `claimboundary.prompt.md:1046`).
  4. `GENERATE_QUERIES` says "do not repeat queries" but `generateResearchQueries` (`research-query-stage.ts:137`) receives no prior-query list. One repeated query string per run (3/2/2/2 occurrences). R1's repeats ran once with a weekly window and once unrestricted, then hit the cache; `query_budget_exhausted` fired; the contradiction pass admitted one contradicting item.
  5. Freshness: Stage 1 labelled R1's 2022-contract claim `current_snapshot`; the refuting queries about the 2024/2025 dispute ran with `dateRestrict=w` and returned one rejected result each. R3's decision-date queries with `w` returned German municipal pages (19 of 26 rejected in one iteration), while its monthly windows retained 38 of 54 results. Weekly windows were unproductive in the observed cases; verdict impact is unverified without matched unrestricted searches. `dateRestrict` is not persisted in the job JSON (only in `search-cache.db`).
  6. No query in 53 research searches (R1–R3) targeted the audit office; `expectedEvidenceProfile` omitted audit sources. EFK content entered only via press (blick, srf, watson) and was then weighed (R3 CB_03 contradicts, 5 items).
  7. `extractResearchEvidence` forces `relevantClaimIds=[targetClaim.id]` (`research-extraction-stage.ts:403`); evidence found while researching a sibling claim is not attributed to the fixed-price claim and cannot be cited directionally for it (R3 GPK-N finding `EV_029`, neutral on AC_08). The reconciler still sees it as pool context.
  8. A second relevance-loss path in R4: GAO and CBO reports scored 0.75–0.85 and were capped to 0.35 as `foreign_reaction` (`2026-09-09T09:57:33Z`, `09:58:21Z`); for a US–Swiss FMS contract that classification is questionable.
- **Cause 3, language changes retrieval — supported for composition, not for direction.** EN input: 1 Swiss-media result of 70 raw (DE: 16 of 42); no Swiss media in the pool and one Swiss source, the admin.ch English release (2 supporting items); manufacturer page f35.com selected five times; Wikipedia 16 raw hits. Both languages reached `highly_misleading`, 22 points apart (30 vs 52). Both supplementary language lanes are off; `sourceNativeSupplementaryLane` is a no-op scaffold without a planner (`research-orchestrator.ts:1903`).

**Recommendations (none implemented; reviewer ranking adopted):**
1. Repair preliminary-evidence retention and Stage 1 source reuse together (impact high): carry preliminary items into the remap/seed step with provenance, and let Stage 2 re-extract already-fetched text against the final claims. Do not map by rough-claim position or seed blindly; the association must stay an LLM decision.
2. Make clustering degradation visible and reconcile final counts (impact high for report trust): the `boundary-clustering-stage.ts:118` catch path falls back to one boundary without a warning; `meta.evidenceBalance` is computed before the applicability filter.
3. Give the query generator prior queries and their outcomes (impact medium, cost low); raise `perClaimQueryBudget` only after measuring remaining gaps.
4. Investigate the relevance classifier's missing inputs (iteration intent, `expectedEvidenceProfile`) before any prompt wording change; do not exempt refuting-lane results from the floor wholesale, a refuting query can still return noise.
5. Review Stage 1 freshness labelling (`CLAIM_EXTRACTION_PASS2`) against the claim's temporal question; no calendar rule.
6. Design an LLM reassessment step for useful sibling evidence (association and direction together); do not copy neutral sibling items into directional arrays mechanically.
7. Diagnostic accounting: persist `dateRestrict`/`freshnessWindow` and skip reasons on `SearchQuery`/sources; separate already-fetched skips from fetch failures (`fetchRejected` currently mixes top-N exclusion, skips and failures); count source-reliability lookups.
8. Defer cap/provider tuning (`maxSourcesPerIteration`, `relevanceTopNFetch`, Google CSE primary) and any article-level misleadingness mechanism; exploratory, Captain approval required, and the banner's actual text should be assessed first.
- Wording for a clean demonstration (Captain must approve any analysis input before use): keep the operative predicate as the claim's predicate rather than a price-plus-qualifier conjunction, e.g. "Der von der Schweiz mit den USA vereinbarte Preis von 6,035 Milliarden Franken für 36 F-35A ist ein rechtlich verbindlicher Pauschal-Festpreis, der Mehrkosten durch Teuerung ausschliesst."
