Hello, I’m Robert Schaub. I spent 38 years in software engineering and project leadership—30 at Phonak/Sonova, helping people hear better. I founded FactHarbor Verein, a Swiss non-profit, and built FactHarbor’s evidence-based fact-checking Alpha and Our AI Charter Runtime, a runnable decision-gateway proof of concept.

My motivation is societal: facts are an essential foundation of democracy and justice. I want AI systems to strengthen evidence-based decision-making and accountability. For consequential answers, that means checking authority and evidence before release and giving affected people a record they can inspect and challenge.

The FactHarbor Alpha assesses public claims against supporting and opposing evidence. Here is a report from the public Alpha. It examines a blanket heat-pump recommendation for a Winterthur home and records supporting and opposing evidence, sources, limitations, uncertainty and its verdict. For the prototype, selected claim reports will be prepared in advance for the decision gateway. A later product could acquire new evidence under explicit authority and limits.

Evidence-Gated Agents will test this path. An AI may draft a consequential answer, but the gateway outside the model decides whether current authority and declared evidence support releasing that exact answer. If the gateway blocks it, the request may be narrowed—or the person may decline. Any new answer must be based on current authority and evidence and pass every check again before release. If released, the recipient receives an inspectable receipt linking the answer, authority, evidence, decision and outcome.

Beyond the prototype, I want to develop evidence-gated decision-making applications and services for companies, public bodies, newsrooms and civil society. With permission, they could weigh an organisation’s policies, contracts and records alongside public evidence. People affected by an answer should be able to understand it, challenge it, and seek correction or independent review.

I built both foundations and can integrate them directly. The Prototype Fund’s coaches and other participants can challenge my assumptions, improve the recipient experience, and connect me with practitioners who can test whether the receipt is understandable and whether AI is justified.

My vision is a free and fair society in which technology strengthens democracy because consequential decisions remain authorised, evidence-grounded and open to challenge.
